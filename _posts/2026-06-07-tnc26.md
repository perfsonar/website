If you are attending [TNC26](https://tnc26.geant.org), in Helsinki, Finland, this June 2026,
you are kindly invited to join in a few different sessions.

You'll probably see a few of perfSONAR developers and users at the: 
 * [network latency](https://tnc26.geant.org/programme/#Monday/all/s1181) BoF, on Tuesday at 9:00 ;
 * [powering data intensive science](https://tnc26.geant.org/programme/#Monday/all/s1192) community hub, on Wednesday 10th at 14:00 ;
 * [network visibility and monitoring](https://tnc26.geant.org/programme/#Monday/all/s1077) session, on Wednesday 10th at 16:00 ;
 * [federated measurement sharing with MetrANOVA](https://tnc26.geant.org/programme/#Monday/all/c1149) workshop, on Thursday at 9:00 and at 11:00 ;

and most probably at other places and times too.

See you there!
